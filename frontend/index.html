How it works

Report · Connect · Respond

One accountable path from a resident's report to a verified outcome. Every step records who acted and when.

01

Report

Residents describe the problem in English, हिन्दी or मराठी, pin the location and optionally add a photo. They get a reference and a private tracking code.

02

Connect

Related reports are grouped into incidents, matched to compatible assets nearby, and connected to the recorded utility network — with reasons shown for every match.

03

Respond

Officers trace the network, contact the authorised operator, approve a plan, record progress and close the case only with accepted proof.

Underground utility intelligence

Click a pipe. See what it connects to.

Officers select a water main, sewer, cable or gas line on the map and see the network as it is recorded — not as it looks.

  • Recorded connections upstream and downstream, traced by a deterministic graph engine.
  • Sources and candidate isolation points found along recorded paths, with verification status.
  • Operator contacts with source and last-verified date. Fictional demo contacts are marked as such.
  • Gaps stated plainly: missing links, unverified records and approximate geometry are flagged.
  • Lines crossing on a map are never treated as a connection.
AI assistance

Help with the work, not decisions about it

AI suggests, explains and drafts. Deterministic code computes distances, network paths, permissions and workflow rules, and people approve every consequential step.

Multilingual reporting

Category suggestions and follow-up questions in English, Hindi and Marathi. The citizen's own words are always kept; translations are stored separately after the citizen reviews them.

Utility Copilot

Officers ask "where does this pipe connect?" or "which valves are upstream?". Answers come from the recorded network and cite asset IDs, or say what data is missing.

Incident briefings

A structured summary of what was reported, the candidate assets, history and data gaps — facts kept apart from hypotheses, with a coordination checklist.

When no AI provider is available, rule-based classification and record-based answers keep every workflow running.

Three portals, one record

The right view for each role

Citizen

Report an issue, follow its status in plain language, get notified of changes. Only your own reports are visible to you.

Officer

Priority queue, live map with utility layers, network tracing, operator coordination, action plans, evidence and verification.

Administrator

Users and roles, infrastructure data sources and validated imports, public-data discovery, data quality and audit history.

Trust and accountability

Honest about what the data shows

Every infrastructure record carries its provenance, and the interface says when something is unverified or fictional.
