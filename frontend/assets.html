How the rules work
  • A · Repeated asset complaints — at least N open reports matched to one asset within W days.
  • B · Repeat after maintenance — a report matched to an asset within L days after a recorded repair/replacement/cleaning. A possible recurrence, not proof the work failed.
  • C · Critical infrastructure incident — an open incident of sufficient severity linked to an asset marked critical.
  • D · Geographic concentration — several open incidents of related categories close together. They are not merged automatically.
  • E · Overdue inspection — last inspection older than the asset's interval. Only evaluated when both values exist.
  • F · Conflicting open actions — open plans on different incidents targeting the same asset.

Thresholds are in data/civicmind_config.json → risk_rules.
